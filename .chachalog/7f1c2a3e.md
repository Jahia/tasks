Say which language a publication request is for, on the task card and in the list.

Content is published one language at a time, so asking to publish the same pages in English and in French raises two separate requests and two separate tasks. Until now those two tasks looked the same on the board: same name, same content, same people, nothing to tell them apart. The summary line did start with a language, but it was the language of the person reading the board, not the language of the request, so it could say "en" on a French request. The card now carries the requested language as its own label, and the summary line states the language that was actually asked for.
