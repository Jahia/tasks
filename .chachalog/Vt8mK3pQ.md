Added a switcher to My Tasks so you can read your tasks either as a board or as a sortable list.

The list shows every task in one table, with columns for due date, priority, how long it has been waiting, who it is assigned to and its state, and sorts on the column header you click. The scope, search and sort you already had stay as they were when you switch, and the view you chose is remembered for your next visit.
